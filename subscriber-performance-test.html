iPhone · Safari

Android · Chrome

Learning Mode · Song stopwatch

Tap Start timing, play the song at your normal pace, then tap Stop & save. Only the total time is saved; scroll by hand whenever convenient. Your scroll movements and pauses are not recorded.

Next time, open this song in Playing and press Scroll. From the top, the chart scrolls steadily to the bottom over your saved duration at 100% speed. Opening a song never starts scrolling automatically. Use + / − to adjust the speed while playing. Touch the chart to pause, then press Scroll to resume from that position.

Enter a time below to edit the duration, or time the song again. Saved durations and speed adjustments stay on this device and work offline. A cancelled stopwatch keeps your previous saved duration.
