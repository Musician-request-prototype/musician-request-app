Stay connected at your gig
Live requests, volume feedback, saves and show controls need an internet connection: Wi-Fi or cellular. If your tablet has no cellular service, connect it to your phone’s personal hotspot. To read songs without internet, open Performance Settings and prepare this device first.

Add to your Home Screen

For your performer tools, open your main menu and save that page. Share your audience link with fans instead.

iPhone · Safari
Open the page in Safari. Tap Share (or Page Menu → Share), then Add to Home Screen. If offered, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

iPad · Safari
Open the page in Safari, tap Share, then View More if needed, then Add to Home Screen. If offered, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Android · Chrome
Open the page in Chrome. Tap the three-dot menu → Install and create shortcut → Create shortcut → Add. Some versions label this Add to Home screen.

Checking your offline songs…

For the best experience, connect to Wi-Fi or cellular data, including your phone’s personal hotspot. Songs are prepared automatically on each device you use. Keep this app open until ready, then test reopening in airplane mode before a gig.

Sign in to your musician account

Save drafts while planning, then publish dates on your audience’s Shows tab. These dates are separate from your recorded performance history.

Add show

New shows default to 3 hours. Changing the start updates that default until you choose an end time yourself. Saved and pasted end times are kept. An earlier end time means the following day.

Paste your monthly post

Choose the post’s month and year, then paste the text with one show per line. We’ll make an editable review list. Nothing is published until you choose Publish.

For evening bookings such as 6–9, choose Shows start in PM to fill 6 PM–9 PM.
