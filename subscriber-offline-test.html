How offline access works

All downloaded songs, artists, readiness labels, lyrics and chords are available here without internet. The latest completed download replaces the previous copy only after every song has loaded and the library has been checked.

Signing out locks this device copy. Sign back into the same account while online to unlock it. No passwords are stored by the offline library.

Requests, volume feedback, song editing, show controls and recording songs played still require internet. Reconnecting does not upload or change any songs from this reading copy.
